Leftover stock hunting grounds

Shops built like Old Glory, Stylin Online and Rockabilia: online tee retailers that have sold licensed band and pop-culture shirts since the late 90s or 2000s and still run old catalogs, plus a few eil/RareVinyl-style memorabilia dealers. Every site was opened on Oct 21, 2026. The middle column says what I actually saw on the site that makes it a lead. Strong lead means I found a stated founding year or visible old stock; Worth a look means it is the right kind of shop but the evidence is thinner.

Checked and dropped

Gone: interpunk.com (now spam), gemm.com (for sale), loudclothing.com (gambling), rocksax.com (parked), oldschooltees.com (spam), merchandisingplaza.co.uk (now a casino site), superheroden.com (for sale), rock.com (different company now), shopmyband.com (empty), rockerteeshirts.com (403), usakle.com and band-tees.com (down).

Not the right kind of shop (new print-on-demand or label and merch companies, removed from the last version): Liquid Blue, IndieMerch, MerchNow, Backstreet Merch, EMP, Plastic Head, Impericon, Nuclear Blast, Kings Road, Metalomania (opened 2015), Grindstore, Teehunt, TeeShirtPalace, Shirtmandude, Phunky Threads, AnimationShops, Popfunk, and the label webstores and record stores. 991.com now just redirects to RareVinyl.
Where a store's online start date is not stated on its site, the "online since" column is an estimate from its site and history. Check that a shirt is old from the tag and stitching before you buy; long-running shops also restock reprints.
