Old stock hunting list

Websites of original sellers, record shops and memorabilia dealers that still hold unsold licensed tees printed before 2010: comic and movie/TV licensees, convention dealers, toy and comic shops with old catalogs, and old record and memorabilia dealers. There are no sports shops, no used-vintage resellers and no Japanese resale shops. Every site was opened between Oct 21 and Oct 22, 2026. The middle column says what I actually saw that makes it a lead. A "search index" tag means the site blocked my checker, so that evidence comes from Google's copy of the page.

Is there anything like Rockabilia, Old Glory or Stylin Online?

Almost nothing. I pulled the band, pop-culture and TV/movie tee shops listed next to them in 2007–09 web directories (archived copies of DMOZ) and checked 70 domains. Nearly all are dead, parked or now a different business (the list is at the bottom). The ones still trading that work the same way are:
OnHollywood / Star500: wholesale concert tees since 1996, still on old store software and still listing SKUs for 2000s bands.
Official Store: Old Glory's own wholesale side, with 2000s stock counted by size.
Liquid Blue: sells boxes from its own warehouse clean-outs.
For Stylin-type pop culture, the only other survivor is WickedCoolStuff, whose site has been frozen since about 2009. Beyond these, the old stock is in the back rooms of record shops and comic shops, which is why the call-them leads below matter.
