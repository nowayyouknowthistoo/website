Old stock hunting list

Websites of original sellers that still hold unsold licensed tees printed before 2010: comic and movie/TV licensees, convention dealers, toy and comic shops with old catalogs, and old record and memorabilia dealers. There are no sports shops, no used-vintage resellers and no Japanese resale shops. Every site was opened between Oct 21 and Oct 22, 2026. The middle column says what I actually saw that makes it a lead. A "search index" tag means the site blocked my checker, so that evidence comes from Google's copy of the page.
